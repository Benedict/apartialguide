# To Bomb Sight, University of Portsmouth

**To:** Dr Kate Jones, project director of Bomb Sight (University of Portsmouth when the project ran, 2011–13). If she has moved, the Department of Geography at Portsmouth, or the project's contact at bombsight.org.
**Subject:** Permission to use Bomb Sight's bomb locations in a free, non-commercial walk of Croydon

---

Dear Dr Jones,

I am making _A Partial Guide_, a non-commercial art piece: a walk of Croydon told as a text adventure, where a narrator called Guide describes each place only from open data, with every fact credited to where it came from. It is free to use at https://apartialguide.com, and its source is public at https://github.com/Benedict/apartialguide.

Bomb Sight's terms allow its material for non-commercial individual, academic and research use. A public walk goes beyond that, so I would like to ask your permission. It would be for a small part of it:

- **What:** the bomb locations from the aggregate night-time Bomb Census, 7 October 1940 to 6 June 1941, within the London Borough of Croydon. For each point, only what the census records: where it fell and the kind of bomb.
- **How it would be used:** when the reader stands within a few metres of a recorded point, Guide would say so, plainly, for example: "A high-explosive bomb was recorded falling here, between October 1940 and June 1941." Nothing would be added to what the census records. No historical maps or scans would be shown, so no National Archives material is involved.
- **Credit:** on the piece's About page, in whatever words you ask for, with a link to bombsight.org.
- **Non-commercial:** the piece is free, carries no advertising, charges no fee and is not sold. It may in future ask readers for voluntary support towards its running costs; if you would rather it didn't while using Bomb Sight, please say.
- **One thing to be open about:** the walk is served in small pieces that the reader's browser fetches as they go, so the points would travel inside those pieces, though they would not be offered as a download.

If it is easier to send the points as a file (CSV, GeoJSON or a shapefile), any form is fine. I would be glad to send you the finished piece, or to change the wording if you would prefer the census to be described differently.

Thank you for Bomb Sight. It is a remarkable piece of work.

With best wishes,

Benedict

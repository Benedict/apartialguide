# To Bomb Sight, University of Portsmouth

**To:** Dr Kate Jones, project director of Bomb Sight (University of Portsmouth when the project ran, 2011–13). If she has moved, the Department of Geography at Portsmouth, or the project's contact at bombsight.org.
**Subject:** Permission to use Bomb Sight's bomb locations in a non-commercial art piece about Addiscombe, Croydon

---

Dear Dr Jones,

I am making _A Partial Guide_, a non-commercial art piece: a text "street view" of Addiscombe, in Croydon, where a narrator called Guide describes each place only from open data, with every fact credited to where it came from. It is free to use at https://benedict.github.io/osm-text-adventure/, and its source is public at https://github.com/benedict/osm-text-adventure.

Bomb Sight's terms say that permission is needed from the project, through the University of Portsmouth, to use the bomb site locations dataset. I would like to ask for that permission, for a small part of it:

- **What:** the bomb locations from the aggregate night-time Bomb Census, 7 October 1940 to 6 June 1941, within the box 51.3689–51.3905 N, 0.0916–0.0570 W (Addiscombe and its edges). For each point, only what the census records: where it fell and the kind of bomb.
- **How it would be used:** when the reader stands within a few metres of a recorded point, Guide would say so, plainly, for example: "A high-explosive bomb was recorded falling here, between October 1940 and June 1941." Nothing would be added to what the census records. No historical maps or scans would be shown, so no National Archives material is involved.
- **Credit:** on the piece's About page, as your terms ask: "Bomb Sight by University of Portsmouth is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License", with a link to bombsight.org.
- **Non-commercial:** the piece is free, carries no advertising, and is not sold.
- **One thing to be open about:** the piece is a single web page that carries its data with it, so the points within that box would be inside the published page, though not offered as a download.

If it is easier to send the points as a file (CSV, GeoJSON or a shapefile), any form is fine. I would be glad to send you the finished piece, or to change the wording if you would prefer the census to be described differently.

Thank you for Bomb Sight. It is a remarkable piece of work.

With best wishes,

Benedict
